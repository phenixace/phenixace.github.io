GC-SNTK formulates graph condensation as kernel ridge regression. A structure-based neural tangent kernel captures graph topology and removes the need to repeatedly train a graph neural network in the inner optimization loop.

The experiments measure condensation speed and downstream prediction performance on the condensed graphs.
