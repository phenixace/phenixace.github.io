This work introduces a Molecular Perturbation framework that creates syntax-valid structural variants under controlled graph edit distance. Even a single edit can substantially reduce performance on common molecular tasks, revealing a narrow local trust region and fragile sensitivity to structural change.

The experiments further show that in-context tuning with structurally similar molecules can partially expand that trust region, suggesting a practical direction for more robust molecular language models.
