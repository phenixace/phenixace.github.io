Mol-R1 trains explicit long-chain reasoning models for text-based molecule generation. Prior Regulation via In-context Distillation (PRID) produces paired reasoning traces guided by chemical constraints.

Molecular Iterative Adaptation (MoIA) alternates supervised fine-tuning with reinforced policy optimization. The paper evaluates the resulting models on text-based molecular reasoning and generation.
