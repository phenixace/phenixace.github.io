MFE-NER combines glyph and phonetic features to recognize Chinese named entities when characters are replaced with visually or phonetically similar alternatives. Five-Stroke components represent character structure, and a phonetic encoding represents pronunciation similarity.

The features augment a pre-trained language model. Experiments evaluate character-substitution detection and overall Chinese NER performance.
