In-Context Molecule Adaptation (ICMA) trains language models to connect molecules with textual descriptions using retrieved examples. The pipeline combines cross-modal retrieval, post-retrieval re-ranking, and In-Context Molecule Tuning.

BM25 caption retrieval and molecular graph retrieval supply examples. Sequence reversal and random walks re-rank those examples before tuning the model for molecule-caption translation.
