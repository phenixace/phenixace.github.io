MolReFlect aligns molecular substructures with phrases in their descriptions. A teacher model proposes detailed alignments, and In-Context Selective Reflection lets a student model select between the initial and revised annotations.

The student learns from these alignments through Chain-of-Thought In-Context Molecule Tuning. Experiments on ChEBI-20 evaluate the approach for molecule-caption translation.
