Speak-to-Structure evaluates molecular generation from open-ended natural-language instructions. Its S2-Bench benchmark allows multiple valid molecules for a design brief and includes molecule editing, optimization, and customized generation.

The study evaluates 31 language models and introduces OpenMolIns for instruction tuning. A tuned Llama3.1-8B outperforms GPT-4o and Claude-3.5 on S2-Bench in the reported experiments.
