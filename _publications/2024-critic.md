Critic-V pairs a Reasoner with a separate Critic for multimodal reasoning. The Reasoner produces a response from visual and textual inputs, and the Critic provides natural-language feedback for revision.

The Critic is trained with Direct Preference Optimization on critiques ranked by a rule-based reward. The study evaluates reasoning accuracy and efficiency across eight benchmarks.
