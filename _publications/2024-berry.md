LLaMA-Berry combines Monte Carlo Tree Search with iterative self-refinement for mathematical reasoning. A Pairwise Preference Reward Model compares candidate solutions, and Enhanced Borda Count converts those comparisons into a global ranking.

The evaluation examines search efficiency and problem-solving performance on benchmarks including GPQA, AIME24, and AMC23.
