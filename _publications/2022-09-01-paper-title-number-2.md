This work uses Fusion-in-Decoder to combine topic information with dialogue history for multi-turn conversation. It compares three channel settings for encoding these inputs.

Experiments on the Chinese NaturalConv dataset examine news-grounded conversations. Combining a whole-passage channel with an additional history channel gives competitive results while accommodating long input texts.
