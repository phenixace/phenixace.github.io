STAMP stands for Sequential Tree Attachment-based Motif-Preserving representation. This molecular language encodes motif identity, attachment sites, and fragment-tree topology. It provides reusable chemical fragments and addressable subtrees for structural editing.

Across molecular generation, text-to-molecule translation, property prediction, activity-cliff prediction, optimization, and instruction-guided editing, STAMP connects learned fragment representations with executable operations. Its deterministic compiler also allows frozen language models to act on indexed molecular fragments instead of rewriting complete molecular strings.
