MolReGPT uses retrieved examples to translate between molecules and natural-language descriptions. It selects similar molecules and their captions from a local database, then includes them in a language model’s prompt.

The method uses in-context learning without fine-tuning. Experiments evaluate molecule understanding and text-based molecule generation, including comparisons with MolT5-base.
