IAG studies input-aware backdoor attacks on vision-language models used for visual grounding. A text-conditioned UNet produces triggers associated with a specified target object description.

The evaluation covers models including LLaVA, InternVL, and Ferret, and benchmarks including RefCOCO, RefCOCO+, RefCOCOg, Flickr30k Entities, and ShowUI. It measures attack success, clean-input accuracy, and transfer across models and datasets.
