ChemLLM is a chemistry language model accompanied by ChemData, an instruction-tuning dataset, and ChemBench, a benchmark covering nine chemistry tasks.

The framework adapts structured chemical knowledge for dialogue. Its evaluation compares performance on chemical tasks and general-language tasks with models including GPT-4.
