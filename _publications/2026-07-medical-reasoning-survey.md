This survey connects clinical practice with computational methods through a dual-view framework. On the clinical side, it develops a five-level competency scheme based on Miller's Pyramid. On the computational side, it links deductive, inductive, and abductive reasoning to common medical goals and tasks.

The work also introduces a five-level medical reasoning benchmark and evaluates 18 state-of-the-art models, highlighting complementary strengths and persistent gaps in diagnosis, decision support, dialogue, hallucination, and grounding.
