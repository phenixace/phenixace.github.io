Chem-R learns chemical reasoning through three training stages: chemical foundation training, reasoning-protocol distillation, and multi-task Group Relative Policy Optimization.

The model is evaluated on molecular and reaction tasks. The reported gains over leading language models, including Gemini-2.5-Pro and DeepSeek-R1, reach up to 32% on molecular tasks and 48% on reaction tasks.
